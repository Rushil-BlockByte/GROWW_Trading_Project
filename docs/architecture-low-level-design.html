Architecture And Low Level Design

How Data Moves Through The Trading Scanner

Think of the system as a read-only data pipeline. It connects to Kite, builds clean market context, scores a setup, applies safety and risk gates, and then lets you record only a paper-trade decision.

Low Level Design Flow

1. Start Stream Flow

  1. User clicks Start Stream.

    The dashboard calls the stream API.

  2. Safety checks run first.

    Credentials, market hours, and access token are checked.

  3. Instrument master loads.

    The app downloads NSE and NFO instruments from Kite.

  4. Indicators are warmed.

    Recent 1-minute candles are fetched so EMA, RSI, ATR, and volume are ready.

  5. WebSocket connects.

    Index ticks arrive first; option-chain subscriptions follow around ATM.

2. Tick To Decision Flow

  1. Live tick arrives.

    The raw tick is normalized into a clean internal format.

  2. State and candles update.

    Latest price, OI, bid/ask, and 1m/5m/15m candles are refreshed.

  3. Indicators update.

    VWAP, EMAs, ATR, RSI, volume, opening range, support, and resistance refresh.

  4. Option context updates.

    NIFTY, BANKNIFTY, and FINNIFTY option rows are calculated separately.

  5. Scanner scores the setup.

    The app explains confirmed, forming, no trade, or blocked states.

3. Paper Trade Flow

  1. Scanner must confirm.

    No confirmed signal means no paper trade button action.

  2. Selected contract must be tradable.

    The option needs live LTP, volume, OI, and acceptable spread.

  3. Risk size is calculated.

    Quantity uses trading capital, risk percent, live option LTP, stop, and lot size.

  4. Journal entry is saved.

    The app saves paper trade details and rule checks for review.

4. No Trade Flow

  1. Data is incomplete or stale.

    Blank indicators, stale ticks, or disconnected WebSocket block decisions.

  2. Market setup is weak.

    Sideways price, low volume, poor momentum, or bad risk-reward can block.

  3. Option is not safe enough.

    Wide spread, low OI, low volume, or unavailable contract blocks the trade.

  4. Explanation is shown.

    The AI explanation layer translates the reason into plain language.
