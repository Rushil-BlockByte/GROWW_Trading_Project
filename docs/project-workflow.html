GROWW Trading Project

Simple Workflow Guide

The project is a read-only and paper-trading scanner for Indian index options. Its job is to collect live market data, calculate context, explain whether a setup is worth watching, and stop you from taking a paper trade when the data or risk is not ready.

Daily Workflow

1 Before 9:15

Open the dashboard, confirm login, check capital/risk settings, and make sure the Kite token is fresh.

2 At 9:15

Start the Zerodha stream. The app loads instruments, warms indicators with recent candles, then waits for live ticks.

3 Read Context

Check market cards, indicators, option chain, liquidity, and system health. If any important block is waiting, do not paper trade.

4 Wait For Setup

The scanner explains whether the setup is forming, confirmed, blocked, or no trade. Use this as a filter, not as blind permission.

5 Record Review

Capture only paper trades or notes, then review the result later using reports and the review workspace.

How Each Block Helps

Market

Market Cards

Shows live LTP, change, VWAP distance, day low/high, trend, and data quality for NIFTY, BANKNIFTY, and FINNIFTY.

Use it for: deciding which index is active enough to watch.

Indicators

Indicator Context

Shows close, VWAP, EMA 9/20/50, ATR 14, RSI 14, relative volume, opening range, support, and resistance.

Use it for: checking trend, momentum, volatility, and key levels.

Scanner

Opportunity Scanner

Combines trend, VWAP, breakout, volume, momentum, option-chain, liquidity, and risk-reward checks.

Use it for: knowing why the app says watch, confirmed, or no trade.

Options

Option Chain

Displays live CE and PE prices, OI, OI change, spread, and liquidity around ATM. NIFTY, BANKNIFTY, and FINNIFTY have their own option tables.

Use it for: picking only liquid contracts with acceptable spreads.

Options

Option Context

Summarizes total CE/PE OI, OI ratios, volume, major OI leaders, support zones, and resistance zones.

Use it for: understanding where option writers may defend levels.

Risk

Risk Dashboard

Shows trading capital, daily loss limit, risk per trade, and live position size using live option LTP and live lot size.

Use it for: preventing oversized paper trades.

Live Feed

Zerodha Stream

Starts and stops the read-only Kite WebSocket. It shows token status, connection, subscription count, tick age, and start gate.

Use it for: confirming live data is actually connected.

Pipeline

Data Pipeline

Shows instrument master count, ATM strike, option universe count, candles, tracked instruments, and data quality.

Use it for: checking if the app has enough raw data to calculate.

Safety

System Health

Shows WebSocket health, last tick age, subscription health, database state, market hours, and whether live orders are disabled.

Use it for: stopping decisions when data is stale or blocked.

Journal

Paper Trade Journal

Records paper trades and notes with signal details, selected option, entry, stop, quantity, and rule violations.

Use it for: building discipline and review history.

Review

Backtests And Reports

Shows sample and saved backtest reports, exports, report links, daily notes, and weekly review records.

Use it for: learning which setups worked over time.

Planning

Daily Plan

Keeps the day focused before trading begins: market conditions, risk rules, allowed setups, and what to avoid.

Use it for: avoiding random trades.

Simple Paper Trade Rule

Do not take a paper trade unless all important blocks are green or ready. A missing indicator, stale tick, blocked market-hours gate, illiquid option, or invalid position size means wait.

  • 1
    Live stream connected

    The WebSocket is connected and latest tick age is fresh.

  • 2
    Indicators populated

    VWAP, EMAs, ATR, RSI, volume, support, and resistance are visible.

  • 3
    Option contract is liquid

    The selected CE/PE has live LTP, OI, volume, and acceptable spread.

  • 4
    Scanner confirms the setup

    The signal is confirmed, not just forming or no trade.

  • 5
    Risk sizing is valid

    Quantity is calculated from live option price and live lot size.

  • 6
    Live orders remain off

    This project is for read-only scanning and paper-trade recording.

What The App Is Not

Not This Instead It Does This
It is not an automatic trading bot. It keeps live order execution disabled and records paper decisions only.
It is not a guaranteed buy/sell system. It gives context, gates, explanations, and risk checks for disciplined review.
It should not trade from blank values. It waits for live ticks and warmed indicators before decision blocks are useful.

Paper-trading and education tool only. Markets carry risk. Use the dashboard as a discipline checklist, not as financial advice.
